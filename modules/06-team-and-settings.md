# Module 06 — Team and Settings

## Page: Team `/projects/:projectId/team`
Sections: member list, roles, invitations, assigned workload.
Actions: invite, change role, revoke access (owner only).
Acceptance: role changes immediately affect permissions.

## Page: Settings `/projects/:projectId/settings`
Sections: project metadata, stack, repository URL, AI development rules, environment labels.
Actions: edit settings, archive project, view decisions.
Acceptance: changes logged and restricted by role.

## Page: Decisions `/projects/:projectId/decisions`
Sections: chronological decisions and rationale.
Actions: add decision, link affected sections/tasks.
Acceptance: decision history remains reviewable.
